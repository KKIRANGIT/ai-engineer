# Case Study Checklist

Back to [Week 18 README](../README.md)

Use this checklist when turning the capstone into a portfolio or interview story.

## Problem

- What real user problem does the product solve?
- Why is AI used here instead of plain rules alone?

## Architecture

- Why did you choose this pattern?
- Why did you avoid a more complex agent architecture?
- Where do retrieval and tools fit?

## Quality

- How do you know the output is useful?
- What eval set exists?
- What failure modes are still present?

## Operations

- What traces or logs are available?
- What guardrails exist?
- How is cost estimated?

## Reflection

- What would you improve next?
- What would you build differently in production?
