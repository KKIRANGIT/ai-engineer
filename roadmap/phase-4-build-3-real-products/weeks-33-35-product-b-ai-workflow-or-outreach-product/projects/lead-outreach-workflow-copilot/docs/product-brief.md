# Product Brief

## Target User

Small sales or growth teams that spend too much time researching leads and drafting first-touch outreach.

## Painful Workflow

The team manually researches a company, infers likely pain points, drafts a first email, and then reviews tone and relevance before sending.

## Product Promise

The copilot should reduce repetitive research and first-draft effort while keeping approval with the human operator.

## Deliberate Scope

- company and persona enrichment from known task inputs
- structured outreach brief
- drafted first-touch email
- approval-required final state
