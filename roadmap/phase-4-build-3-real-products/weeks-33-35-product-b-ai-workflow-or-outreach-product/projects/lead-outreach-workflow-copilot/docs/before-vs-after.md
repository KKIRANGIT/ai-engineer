# Before vs After

## Manual Workflow

- research company context
- infer likely pain point
- write a personalized first email
- review tone and relevance

Estimated time per task: 18 minutes

## Assisted Workflow

- load lead task
- generate structured research brief
- generate first-touch draft
- review and approve

Estimated time per task: 7 minutes

## Main Claim

The product does not eliminate review. It reduces repetitive prep and draft work while preserving a human approval step.
