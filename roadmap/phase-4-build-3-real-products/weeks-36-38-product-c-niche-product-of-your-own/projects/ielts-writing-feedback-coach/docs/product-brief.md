# Product Brief

## Target User

IELTS candidates who already know the exam format but need faster, more structured writing feedback between tutor sessions.

## Painful Workflow

Students often write practice essays but wait too long for detailed rubric-aligned feedback, which slows improvement and wastes practice effort.

## Product Promise

Give the student a fast first-pass critique with rubric-shaped scores, focused weaknesses, and a short next-step study plan.

## Deliberate Scope

- IELTS Writing Task 2 only
- English essays only
- fast feedback between human coaching sessions
- no claim that the tool replaces official scoring
