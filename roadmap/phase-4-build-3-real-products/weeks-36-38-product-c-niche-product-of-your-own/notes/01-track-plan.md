# Product C Track Plan

## Objective

Choose one niche-specific product bet and judge it rigorously enough to influence future direction.

## Suggested Flow

1. Read the track README.
2. Complete the exercises in week order.
3. Read the project brief, cost notes, and roadmap docs.
4. Run the project tests and inspect the feedback output.
5. Rewrite the strategic review for a niche you are personally considering.
