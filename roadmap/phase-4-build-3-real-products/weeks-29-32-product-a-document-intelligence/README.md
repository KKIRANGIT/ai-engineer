# Weeks 29-32: Product A - Document Intelligence

Back to [Phase 4](../README.md)

## Goal

Build a grounded document assistant for a specific real-world use case.

## Good Niches

- legal summaries
- company policies
- insurance documents
- financial reports
- internal SOP assistants

## Required Capabilities

- file upload
- parsing and chunking
- metadata-aware retrieval
- citations
- question answering
- usage tracking

## What To Optimize

- retrieval quality
- hallucination resistance
- upload reliability
- source clarity

## Deliverables

- product demo
- eval questions
- user feedback from at least 3-5 testers
- case study
