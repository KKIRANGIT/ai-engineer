## Access Reviews

Managers must review privileged access for their team members once every quarter and confirm whether each permission is still required.

## Approval

Any request for admin access to customer systems requires approval from the employee's manager and the Security lead before credentials are issued.

## Temporary Access

Temporary elevated access must expire automatically within seven days unless a Security lead records a written extension.
