## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely up to three days per week after completing their first ninety days of employment.

## Availability

Employees approved for remote work must be reachable in Slack and email between 10:00 a.m. and 4:00 p.m. local time on scheduled workdays.

## Travel During Remote Days

Employees may not work remotely from another country for more than ten calendar days without written approval from People Operations and Security.
