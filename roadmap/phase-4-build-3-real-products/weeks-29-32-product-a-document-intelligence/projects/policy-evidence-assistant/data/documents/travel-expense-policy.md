## Receipt Rules

Employees must submit itemized receipts for any individual travel expense above seventy-five dollars.

## Submission Window

Travel reimbursement requests should be submitted within fifteen calendar days after the trip ends.

## Airfare Changes

Flight changes requested for personal convenience are not reimbursable unless approved in writing by the employee's department head before travel.
