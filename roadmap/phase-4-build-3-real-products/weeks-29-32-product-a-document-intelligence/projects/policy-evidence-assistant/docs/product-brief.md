# Product Brief

## Target User

Operations managers and employees who need quick, trustworthy answers from internal policy documents.

## Painful Workflow

People waste time searching multiple policy documents manually, then still feel unsure whether they found the current answer.

## Core Product Promise

The assistant should answer policy questions with visible evidence and clear uncertainty instead of generic policy chat.

## Deliberate Scope

- controlled markdown corpus
- internal policy and operations questions
- grounded responses with citations
- no free-form open-domain answers
