# Product A Track Plan

## Objective

Build a narrow document-intelligence product with a visible trust loop and product evidence.

## Suggested Flow

1. Read the track README.
2. Complete the exercises in week order.
3. Inspect the sample documents and product brief.
4. Run the project tests and review the retrieval debug output.
5. Extend the eval questions and feedback notes for your own niche.

## What "Done" Looks Like

- the niche is specific
- the corpus is representative
- retrieval is inspectable
- grounded answers show evidence clearly
- tester feedback is recorded
