# Resume Bullet Guide

Strong bullets usually contain three layers:

- the problem or system context
- the action you took
- the result, quality gain, or technical significance

Weak bullet:

- built an AI app with Node.js

Stronger bullet:

- built a retrieval-backed policy assistant that grounded answers with citations and evaluation prompts so compliance teams could verify responses more quickly

If you do not have a numeric result, use a specific system or workflow result instead of inventing metrics.
