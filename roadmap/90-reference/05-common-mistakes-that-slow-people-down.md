# Common Mistakes That Slow People Down

Back to [Roadmap Index](../README.md)

## Why This Reference Matters

Many roadmap failures are predictable. This reference is here so you can recognize and interrupt the patterns early.

## High-Impact Mistakes

- consuming tutorials without shipping
- starting agents too early
- using frameworks before direct APIs
- building generic chatbots with no clear value
- ignoring evals because the demo looks good
- ignoring cost until users arrive
- skipping auth, billing, and operations
- building too many small apps and finishing none

## What These Mistakes Usually Mean

### Consuming without shipping

Usually means:

- fear of implementation
- comfort in passive learning

### Starting agents too early

Usually means:

- chasing complexity before understanding simpler patterns

### Using frameworks before direct APIs

Usually means:

- hiding weak understanding behind abstractions

### Building generic chatbots

Usually means:

- weak problem selection
- weak product framing

## Recovery Questions

If you feel stuck, ask:

- am I learning or avoiding
- am I building something specific or just touching tools
- am I finishing projects or only starting them
- am I measuring quality or just admiring output

## Expert Notes

### The most common slowdown is not technical

It is often weak focus and weak execution discipline.

### Avoid complexity theater

A simpler finished system usually teaches and signals more than a more complicated unfinished one.
