# Recommended Portfolio Sequence

Back to [Roadmap Index](../README.md)

## Why This Reference Matters

Portfolio strength is not only about project count. It is about sequence and narrative.

The right order helps your proof compound.

## Recommended Sequence

1. Foundation full-stack app
2. Grounded AI document app
3. Full AI SaaS product
4. Two or three niche products with stronger market focus

This sequence works because each step proves a stronger capability than the last:

- build basics
- AI grounding
- productization
- market-aware differentiation

## What This Sequence Avoids

It avoids:

- many unrelated mini apps
- too much repetition of the same product pattern
- shallow novelty instead of progression

## Expert Notes

### A portfolio should tell a growth story

The sequence should make your evolution visible.

### Stronger later projects depend on earlier discipline

Skipping foundational proof often weakens later credibility.
