# Bottlenecks and Risks

## Likely First Bottlenecks

- AI provider latency on long support analyses
- repeated retrieval or summarization on the same dataset
- background queue backlog during peak uploads
- expensive dashboard queries when usage history grows

## Risks That Matter Early

- tenant scoping mistakes leak data across workspaces
- missing feature flags make risky launches hard to contain
- weak usage accounting makes pricing and quota changes inaccurate
- poor observability hides where the workflow actually breaks
