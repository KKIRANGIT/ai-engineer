# Product Brief

## Target User

Support leads at small SaaS companies who need faster triage and better visibility into risky queues.

## Core Workflow

1. Sign in to a workspace.
2. Upload or select a support dataset.
3. Trigger AI analysis for routing and escalation risks.
4. Review the analysis and assign follow-up actions.

## Paid Value

Paid plans unlock larger analysis volumes, multi-seat access, and premium queue insights.

## Deliberate Scope Limits

- no enterprise SSO
- no custom invoicing
- no multi-region data routing
